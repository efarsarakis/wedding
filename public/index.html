Getting there

Travel to Crete

Flying in Heraklion (HER)

Heraklion is your airport — Lentas sits about 75 km south, an hour and three-quarters' drive through the Asterousia mountains. Chania (CHQ) is technically an option but adds three hours of driving; don't.

  • easyJet and Jet2 both fly direct to Heraklion through September — easyJet from Edinburgh, Gatwick, Luton, Manchester and Bristol; Jet2 from Edinburgh, Glasgow, Manchester, Leeds Bradford, Birmingham and Stansted.
  • Both release seats about a year ahead — September 2027 dates go on sale over the coming weeks, so book as soon as they appear. Jet2 fares include bags; easyJet is cheaper stripped back.
  • Year-round connections via Athens (ATH) with Aegean or SKY Express — under an hour for the hop.

Once you land

Hiring a car is the way — Lentas is a beautifully remote fishing village and the drive down (via Agioi Deka) is half the experience. Book ahead; UK licences are accepted.

Prefer not to drive? Once you're in Lentas, everything is on foot. Taxis to and from Lentas can be arranged, but it's remote enough that they're more faff than they're usually worth; if you're not hiring a car, tell us and we'll pair you with someone who is.

Our island

Explore Crete

The queen's stone menagerie

Find all three petrified guardians: the lion headland above the village, the elephant rock on the coastal path east, and the crocodile of Cape Trafoulas beyond. Below the lion lie the ruins of ancient Lebena and its healing sanctuary of Asclepius.

Matala & the beaches

The famous cave-carved bay is under an hour away; closer still, Trachoulas and Diskos reward a short drive and scramble.

Gortyn & Phaistos

Roman capital of Crete and a great Minoan palace, both on the road down from Heraklion — stop en route.

Heraklion & Knossos

The archaeological museum and the palace of the Minotaur's labyrinth — a good day out, or a stop on the way home.

Good questions

FAQ

What should I wear?

Summer fabulous. Linen, colour, a good hat, the shirt you've been saving — dress like the photographs will be looked at for fifty years, but stay cool enough to dance at midnight. The ceremony is outdoors on stone above the Libyan Sea, so choose footwear accordingly. Kilts enthusiastically welcomed; you will not be the only one.

Why a Monday?

Because the venue is ours for the whole week, and a Monday wedding lets you fly in on the Saturday, settle for a day, and still have the rest of the week ahead of you rather than behind you. Take the Monday and Tuesday off; you'll thank us.

How hot will it be, really?

Early September days reach 26–29 °C with strong sun; evenings settle to a lovely 21–23 °C. The ceremony runs in the evening for exactly this reason. The sea is at its warmest all year — bring swimmers.

Do I need to come to all three days?

Not at all. The Monday is the heart of it; Sunday and Tuesday are there so that a long journey buys more than one evening together.

Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation names everyone in your party. If your circumstances have changed, just write to us — we'll do our best.

Are children welcome?

It's an adults' evening at heart — a late ceremony, a long dinner and a very late party. Children aren't banned: if yours truly can't be left, they're welcome and we'll make sure they're fed and looked after. But if you can arrange for them to stay home, we'd gently encourage it — and you'll enjoy the raki more.

I don't speak Greek — will I manage?

Completely. English is spoken everywhere tourists go in Crete, menus are bilingual, and half the wedding party will happily translate the other half's speeches.

What if flights or plans fall through?

Things happen, especially with international travel. Tell us as soon as you know and we'll adjust — no guilt, ever. Travel insurance is strongly recommended when you book.
